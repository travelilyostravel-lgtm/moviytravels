Requirements

  1. A Tourism Committee guide licence or a guide diploma — enter the number and upload a photo.
  2. Fluent in at least one foreign language.
  3. Main photo: in classic (suit, white shirt) or national dress, a quality portrait with the face clearly visible.
  4. Photos of yourself — at least 3.
  5. Photos with travellers — at least 10 (they prove your experience and appear in your gallery; only with the travellers' consent).
  6. At least 1 year of experience with travellers.
  7. Punctual, neat and polite.
  8. Never make direct deals with clients who came through Moviy Nomad.
