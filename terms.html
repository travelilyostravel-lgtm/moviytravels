Booking and payment terms

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Booking

A booking request on the website is not yet a contract. The manager confirms availability, the programme and the final price in writing (WhatsApp, Telegram or e-mail). The booking is confirmed after the deposit is paid.

Prices

Prices on the website are indicative, per person, and depend on dates, group size, hotel category and transport. The final price is the one confirmed by the manager.

Payment

Deposit: 30% of the trip price to confirm the booking. The remaining amount — no later than 14 days before the trip starts. Payment by bank transfer (UZS or USD/EUR) or by a secure card link sent by the manager.

Cancellation by the traveller

More than 30 days before the trip — full refund minus bank fees. 30–15 days — 50% of the deposit is kept. Less than 14 days — the cost of services already booked and non-refundable (hotels, tickets) is kept. Requests to cancel must be sent in writing.

Changes

We try to accommodate changes to dates or the programme. Any extra costs from hotels, transport or tickets are paid by the traveller.

Our responsibilities

We provide the services described in the confirmed programme. If a service cannot be provided for reasons beyond our control (weather, border closures, decisions of authorities), we offer an equal replacement or refund the cost of the unused service.

Travellers' responsibilities

Travellers must have a valid passport and visa (if required), follow local laws and the guide's safety instructions. We strongly recommend travel insurance.
