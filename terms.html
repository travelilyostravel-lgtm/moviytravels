Booking and payment terms

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Booking

A booking request on the website is not yet a contract. The manager confirms availability, the programme and the final price in writing (WhatsApp, Telegram or e-mail). The booking is confirmed after the deposit is paid.

Prices

Prices on the website are indicative, per person, and depend on dates, group size, hotel category and transport. The final price is the one confirmed by the manager.

Payment

1) Deposit — 30% of the tour price, paid within 3 working days after we send the invoice. The deposit confirms your booking. 2) The remaining 70% is paid no later than 14 days before the tour starts. 100% must be paid before the trip. 3) If you book less than 14 days before the start, 100% is paid at once. 4) Payments go to our company bank account (UZS or USD). We confirm every payment and send your voucher (PDF) after full payment.

If a payment is not made

1) If the deposit is not paid within 3 working days, the request is cancelled with no penalty. 2) If the remaining 70% is not paid on time, we remind you once and give 48 hours. If it is still not paid, the booking is cancelled, the deposit (30%) is not refunded and the place goes to another traveller. 3) If an agent refuses to pay twice, the agent account is suspended and the real cost of services booked in its name is charged.

Cancellation by the traveller

Cancel in writing (e-mail or WhatsApp). Before the trip: 31 days or more — we keep only bank fees and tickets already bought that cannot be refunded; 30–15 days — 15% of the tour price (half of the deposit); 14–8 days — 50%; 7–3 days — 75%; 2 days or less, or no-show — 100%. The rest is refunded within 14 days.

Changes

A traveller's name can be changed free of charge up to 7 days before the trip. The date can be changed once free of charge more than 30 days before the trip. Other changes (hotel, transport, tickets) may cost extra; the traveller pays the difference.

If we cancel

If Moviy Nomad cancels the tour, the traveller gets 100% of the money back or chooses another date.

Force majeure

If flights are cancelled, governments impose restrictions, a natural disaster happens or other events beyond our control occur, the traveller receives a voucher valid for 12 months or a refund minus the costs actually incurred.

Unused services

Services not used by the traveller during the trip (excursions, meals, hotel nights) are not refunded.

Our responsibilities

We provide the services described in the confirmed programme. If a service cannot be provided for reasons beyond our control (weather, border closures, decisions of authorities), we offer an equal replacement or refund the cost of the unused service.

Travellers' responsibilities

Travellers must have a valid passport and visa (if required), follow local laws and the guide's safety instructions. We strongly recommend travel insurance.
