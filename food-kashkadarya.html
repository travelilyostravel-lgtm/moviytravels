Is food in Uzbekistan halal?

Most Uzbek national dishes — plov, shashlik, lagman, samsa — are made with halal meat. Places marked “Halal — confirmed” were checked by Moviy Nomad; elsewhere simply ask the staff.

How do I get to a restaurant?

Open the place and tap “Call Yandex Go taxi”: the app opens with the restaurant as the destination. Taxis in Uzbek cities are inexpensive and arrive in a few minutes.
