FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a visa?

Citizens of many countries can visit Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan visa-free or with a simple e-visa. Tell us your passport country and we will check the current rules for you.

When is the best time to travel?

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are ideal for the cities. Summer is the season for the mountains and lakes of Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan.

Can you organise a private tour?

Yes. We plan private tours for couples and families as well as group tours and programs for travel agencies.

Is the food halal?

Most of the food in the region is halal. We can also plan the program around prayer times and visits to holy sites.

How do I book and pay?

Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram. Once you approve the program, we confirm your booking and send the payment details.

Do you work with travel agencies?

Yes. We provide ground handling and partner rates for agencies and groups.
