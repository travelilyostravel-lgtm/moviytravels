Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Moviy Nomad is a brand of “MOVIY TRAVEL” XK (Bunyodkor shoh ko'chasi 15/1-uy, Toshkent, O'zbekiston, tax ID 311516284). We are responsible for the personal data you share with us through this website, WhatsApp, Telegram or by phone.

What data we collect

Only what is needed to organise your trip: name, phone, e-mail, citizenship, travel dates, number of travellers, the travellers' names and dates of birth, and your wishes. We do not collect passport numbers or card details on the website. If passport details are needed for hotels, trains or border permits, the manager requests them separately through a secure channel.

Why we use it

To answer your request, prepare the programme and price, book hotels, transport and tickets, and stay in touch with you before and during the trip. We do not use your data for anything else without your consent.

Who can see it

Only our booking managers. We pass the minimum necessary data to our partners who provide your services (hotels, drivers, guides, railways). We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

How we protect it

The website works only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Requests are stored in a protected database with access limited to authorised staff. Card payments are made only through secure payment pages of banks — we never see your full card number.

Photos and reviews

If you share photos or a review, we publish them only after moderation, with the name you entered. Location data (GPS) is removed from photos automatically. You can ask us to remove your story at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep booking data for up to 3 years after the trip for accounting and safety reasons, then delete it. Requests that did not turn into a booking are deleted after 1 year.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, correct it or ask us to delete it. Contact us at +998 99 124 42 14 or on Telegram @moviy_safina — we answer within 7 days.
