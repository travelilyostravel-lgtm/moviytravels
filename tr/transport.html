Requirements

  1. Driving licence and at least 3 years of driving experience.
  2. Car from 2015 or newer, air-conditioned, clean inside, insured.
  3. Main photo: in classic or national dress, good quality, face clearly visible.
  4. Car photos — at least 6 (outside, inside, boot).
  5. Photos with travellers — at least 10 (shown in your gallery; only with the travellers' consent).
  6. No smoking in the car, safe driving, punctual.
  7. Never make direct deals with clients who came through Moviy Nomad.
